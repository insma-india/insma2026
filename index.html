The Theme

Global Trends, Homegrown Systems

The theme “Global Trends, Homegrown Systems: Developing India-First Frameworks for Managing the Sport Ecosystem” captures India’s need to engage with international developments in sport management while designing systems grounded in the country’s unique social, cultural, institutional and economic realities.

INSMA 2026 brings together scholars, policymakers, educators, industry leaders, students and practitioners to translate global best practices into locally relevant sport management systems.

The conference focuses on sport performance, participation, policy, economy and social impact while staying grounded in India's cultural, institutional and economic realities.

Thematic Strands

Six tracks for India's sport management future.

Sport Economics, Policy and Governance

This track engages critically with the economic, regulatory and governance dimensions of sport in India, supporting transparent and accountable systems aligned with national and Olympic governance standards.

Technology, Innovation, Society and Culture

This track brings together two converging frontiers in modern sport: the technological transformation of performance and participation, and the social, cultural, and human dimensions of sport systems.

Strategic Sport Leadership and Institutional Development

Anchored in national sport policy reforms and Khelo India objectives, this track addresses governance leadership, organisational strategy, talent pathways, event management and institutional professionalism.

Multidisciplinary Integration in Sport Systems

This track foregrounds integrated, interdisciplinary approaches to sport system development in India, consonant with NEP 2020’s emphasis on holistic education and cross‑disciplinary learning.

Emerging Trends in Sport Management and Sciences

This track examines cutting‑edge developments in performance analytics, biomechanics, exercise physiology, nutrition, recovery, and sports innovation in line with India’s Olympic and high‑performance ambitions.

Rehabilitation Practices, Athlete Health and Welfare

Aligned with Khelo India and national high‑performance mandates, this track focuses on evidence‑based rehabilitation, injury prevention, mental health and long‑term athlete welfare in the Indian context.
